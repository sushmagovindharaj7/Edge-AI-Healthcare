WHY EDGE AI?

Healthcare intelligence at the edge

Analyze health signals close to the user instead of continuously sending raw data to a remote cloud.

⚡

Real-Time Response

Local processing can generate alerts without depending on a round trip to a cloud server.

🧠

AI Risk Prediction

A lightweight browser-based model combines heart rate, oxygen and movement patterns.

🔐

Privacy by Design

The prototype processes simulated readings inside the browser without requiring a patient database.

HOW IT WORKS

From health signals to an instant alert

01 Generate simulated wearable readings automatically.
02 Process the readings locally in the browser.
03 Predict a Normal, Warning or Critical state.
04 Alert the user when an abnormal pattern appears.
